How to answer: Think about your child's behaviour and functioning over the past 4 weeks. Rate each statement honestly based on what you have directly observed. There are no right or wrong answers.
Important: This screening tool is for informational and preventive purposes only. It does not constitute a clinical diagnosis. A high score should be followed up with a qualified RCI-registered psychologist or child development specialist. Individual data is strictly confidential and shared only with you as the parent/guardian.
