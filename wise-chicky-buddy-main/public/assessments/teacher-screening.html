Screening summary

Important notice: This screener is a behavioural observation tool only. It does not constitute a clinical diagnosis of any condition. All flagged concerns must be reviewed by a qualified RCI-registered psychologist or school counsellor before any action is taken.

Results should be interpreted within the full context of the child's development, home environment, and school history. A single elevated domain score does not indicate a clinical condition.

Confidentiality: This report is intended for the reviewing teacher and school counsellor only. Individual child data must not be shared without parental/guardian consent in line with school data protection policy and the DPDP Act 2023 (India).

Clinical basis & methodology
For institutional partners, clinical reviewers, and funding bodies
Original items — no licensed reproduction
All screening items are original observational statements authored by Wise Chicky Pvt. Ltd. No items are verbatim reproductions from any licensed psychometric instrument.
Construct-anchored item writing
Items operationalise behavioural constructs documented in government-mandated and peer-reviewed frameworks, adapted to classroom-observable behaviour for each age band.
Age-adaptive calibration
4 age bands (4–6, 7–9, 10–12, 13–16 yrs). Constructs remain consistent; item language reflects developmentally appropriate behavioural manifestations at each stage.
Validation pathway
Phase 1: Expert review by RCI-registered psychologists. Phase 2: Pilot N=200+ (Jharkhand schools). Phase 3: Reliability & norm development, journal publication.
Clinical reference frameworks
IndianISAA — Indian Scale for Assessment of Autism (2008)
Arya, S. NIEPID / Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Govt. of India. N=1,247, 10 centres, Cronbach's α=0.97. Informs autism indicators domain construct structure and scoring framework.
IndianIASQ — Indian Autism Screening Questionnaire
Government of India. Bilingual (Hindi/English) parent-informant screening instrument. Informs social reciprocity and emotional responsiveness construct language.
InternationalNICHQ Vanderbilt Assessment Scales (2002, rev. 2003)
Wolraich, M.L. American Academy of Pediatrics / NICHQ. DSM-IV-anchored teacher and parent scales for ADHD, ODD, conduct disorder, and anxiety/depression. Informs attention & learning and behaviour & self-regulation domains.
IndianELDSC — Early Learning Difficulty Screening Checklist (2024)
Wise Chicky Pvt. Ltd. 7-domain, 32-item original instrument for dyslexia/SLD screening ages 6–14. Grounded in DSM-5, ICD-11, RPwD Act 2016, and Rao et al. (2021) DALI-DAB validation study.
InternationalDSM-5 — Diagnostic and Statistical Manual, 5th edition (2013)
American Psychiatric Association. Primary diagnostic framework for ADHD, ASD, SLD, ODD, Conduct Disorder, and Depressive/Anxiety Disorders. All domain constructs aligned to DSM-5 criteria.
InternationalICD-11 — International Classification of Diseases, 11th revision (2022)
World Health Organization. Codes 6A02 (ADHD), 6A03 (Developmental Learning Disorder), 6A20 (ASD). Secondary diagnostic framework for global alignment.
Govt. of IndiaRPwD Act 2016 — Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act
Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India. Statutory definitions of Specific Learning Disabilities and Autism Spectrum Disorder used in Indian legal and educational contexts.
IndianRao et al. (2021) — DALI-DAB validation study
Rao, C. et al. Annals of Dyslexia, 71(3), 439–457. Development of Dyslexia Assessment for Languages of India battery. Informs biliteracy context and SLD construct adaptation for Indian schools.
